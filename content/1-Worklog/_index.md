During my internship from **June 15, 2026, to August 14, 2026**, I participated in the **First Cloud AI Journey** program organized by the **AWS Study Group**. Throughout the **nine-week internship**, I studied and practiced the fundamental services of **Amazon Web Services (AWS)** while applying the knowledge I gained to develop a **Library Management System** as part of a team project.

In addition to completing the learning modules and hands-on labs provided by the program, our team followed a complete software development lifecycle, including requirements gathering, proposal preparation, system analysis, system design, implementation, testing, and final product delivery.

The work completed each week is summarized as follows:

**Week 1:** [Introduction to the First Cloud AI Journey program, Module 01, team formation, and project orientation](1.1-week1/)

**Week 2:** [Module 02, selecting the Library Management System project, and preparing the project proposal](1.2-week2/)

**Week 3:** [Module 03, project initialization, development environment setup, and system requirements analysis](1.3-week3/)

**Week 4:** [Module 04, completing the system design and developing the core system functionalities](1.4-week4/)

**Week 5:** [Module 05, implementing core business features, developing RESTful APIs, and collaborating with the frontend team](1.5-week5/)

**Week 6:** [Module 06, completing backend development, integrating system components, and preparing for deployment](1.6-week6/)

**Week 7:** [Deploying the system on AWS, integrating the backend and frontend, performing testing, and optimizing the system](1.7-week7/)

**Week 8:** [Comprehensive system testing, completing project documentation, preparing the project demonstration, and concluding the internship project](1.8-week8/)
