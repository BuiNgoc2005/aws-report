# Reflection on the "AWS Knowledge Challenge"

### Event Objectives

- Review and reinforce AWS knowledge learned throughout the program.
- Create an opportunity for participants to interact and learn from one another.
- Develop teamwork, critical thinking, and quick decision-making skills.

### Event Format

- The competition featured **8 participating teams**.
- Teams answered multiple-choice questions related to AWS.
- The team with the highest total score was declared the winner.

### Key Highlights

- Questions covered AWS concepts learned during the program.
- The competition included both basic and practical AWS scenarios.
- Teams competed through multiple rounds of fast-paced quizzes.

### What I Learned

- Reviewed and reinforced my AWS knowledge.
- Improved my ability to analyze questions and choose the correct answers.
- Strengthened my teamwork and communication skills.

### Application to My Work

- Applied AWS knowledge to hands-on labs and project development.
- Identified areas that require further study.
- Improved teamwork and problem-solving skills under time pressure.

### Event Experience

The competition created an exciting and engaging atmosphere, allowing participants to review AWS knowledge through friendly competition. Discussing questions and making decisions within a limited time helped me improve my critical thinking, quick decision-making, and collaboration with teammates.

#### Lessons Learned

- Regular review is essential for retaining technical knowledge.
- Team discussions often lead to better decisions.
- Competitions like this are a great way to evaluate personal understanding and learn from other teams.
